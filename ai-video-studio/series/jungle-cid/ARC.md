# Jungle CID

A comedy-mystery for a Hindi audience. Things keep going missing in the festival-season village, the evidence keeps pointing at innocent Bhola, and Inspector Chikki and her chaotic assistant Chulbul try to find the real culprit.

## Cast and what each one does in the story

| Character | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Bhola** (sloth bear) | Very innocent and very dumb. Something happens, the clues point at him, and he panics: "Kya main hi chor hoon?" He believes whatever he is told. The audience's heart. |
| **Kallu** (crow) | Knows the village inside out and is deep in the mischief. He uses Bhola's innocence: convinces him he is guilty, then "helps" him (for a samosa). The audience sees his smirk, the investigators do not. |
| **Chikki** (detective fox) | Bold, straightforward, genuinely smart. Notices the small clue that does not fit. Refuses to admit her wrong guesses ("part of the investigation"). |
| **Chulbul** (white rat) | Her sidekick. Cunning in small ways, dumb in big ones. Mistakes and blurted secrets slow the case, and sometimes accidentally crack it. |

## Why people keep watching

- **Dramatic irony:** viewers know more than the investigators (they see Kallu's smirk), so they watch to see when Chikki catches on.
- **Every episode ends on a new clue or a twist,** never a repeat of the same setup.
- **Each case is different** (a different thing goes missing, a different place, a different false suspect), while one bigger story runs underneath.

## Season 1: "Sone ka Laddoo" (10 episodes, 30 seconds each)

**The prize:** the village's golden laddoo, which brings luck for the whole year, is shared at the big village festival. If it goes missing, the festival is ruined. The end card of every episode is simply "Part N coming soon, stay tuned" (no countdown).

**Format of every episode (4 shots, about 7 seconds each):**
1. Hook in the first 2 seconds: a shock or a question.
2. Comedy escalation.
3. A clue or a turn in the investigation.
4. A cliffhanger or a twist, then the end card.

**Two layers of mystery:**
- *Layer 1, known to the audience:* Kallu is stealing and framing Bhola. Viewers watch for when Chikki catches on.
- *Layer 2, secret:* someone is giving Kallu orders. The notes are signed with a golden paw. Small clues are hidden in the background of early episodes (Chulbul licking gold dust, sneaking off, "accidents" that are too lucky), so people rewatch to spot them.

| Ep | Title | Hook and cliffhanger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Kya main hi chor hoon? | Laddoos vanish, Bhola thinks he did it. Three-toed prints and a black feather point at Kallu. |
| 2 | Chulbul ki topi | All the diyas vanish. Chulbul's cap is at the scene and he is arrested. A note: "next target, the golden laddoo". |
| 3 | Sone ka laddoo | The golden laddoo is guarded. At dawn it is fake, and Bhola has gold on his tongue. |
| 4 | Bhola ki hawalaat | Bhola is locked up. Kallu "helps" with a confession that Bhola signs without reading. Chikki notices the paper has three-toed ink marks. |
| 5 | Chikki ka jaal | Chikki lays a flour trap. Chulbul sneezes and ruins it. A new note: "you are getting close". |
| 6 | Raat ka sauda | Chikki follows Kallu at night and hears him report to an unseen boss. She only catches half the voice. |
| 7 | Samose ka dabba | Bhola accidentally finds the real golden laddoo hidden in his own samosa box. Kallu panics. |
| 8 | Jhoota iqbal | Chikki fakes Bhola's confession to flush the boss out. |
| 9 | Chhat par chase | A rooftop chase. The boss's long tail is seen for a second. |
| 10 | Asli chor | The reveal (see below), and a hook into season 2. |

### Planned reveal (spoiler, LOCKED: Chulbul is the boss)
The boss is **Chulbul**. Chikki had been secretly packing to leave for a bigger city because the village was too quiet, and Chulbul, who adores her, staged the thefts to keep her here. Kallu was in it for the samosas. Bhola was framed by accident. The golden laddoo is returned and the festival is saved. The last shot: a new note signed with a different symbol, so season 2 has a real mystery.

### Clue tracker (hide these in the background; never point at them)

| Ep | Hidden clue about Chulbul | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A small gold paw-print sticker on his notebook cover | Shot 3 (arrival) |
| 2 | Licks gold dust off his paw, slips out of the handcuffs too easily | Shot 3 (done) |
| 3 | Falls asleep exactly when the shadow crosses; gold smudges on his paws | Shot 3 |
| 4 | Volunteers to guard Bhola's cell and "brings food"; a tiny suitcase is visible under Chikki's desk | Background |
| 5 | His sneeze ruins the trap right before Kallu would have stepped in it | The sneeze looks like an accident |
| 6 | The boss's voice through a bamboo tube is oddly squeaky and says "सीक्रेट" the same way | Voice only |
| 7 | He is the only one who is not surprised that Bhola found the laddoo | Reaction shot |
| 8 | He volunteers to deliver the fake confession | Dialogue |
| 9 | A long thin tail with a bandage is seen for one second | Chase |
| 10 | Chikki finds her own packed suitcase and the note in his handwriting | Reveal |

Rule: each clue has an innocent explanation in the same shot, so viewers can only spot them on a rewatch or in the comments.
